Quality Control For Mol-JEPA Embeddings¶

We have provided embeddings from the Mol-JEPA foundation model for a subset of the drugs in the Harmonized Drug Dataset (HDD) (version 3.1).
Briefly, Mol-JEPA is a Joint Embedding and Prediction Architecture which is trained to harmonize the latent spaces of existing drug foundation models with existing drug features. For details on how Mol-JEPA was trained, please refer to the preprint.

At present the embeddings we have provided are the <CLS> (classification) tokens provided from the inference step of the Mol-JEPA model (see the Huggingface Model Card).

This notebook presumes you've run download.py, construct_drug_universe.py, and generate_embeddings.py in that order.

def process_atc_code(code_string: str, level: int = 4) -> str:
    """Return the requested ATC hierarchy level (for example N03AX09 -> N03A)."""
    result = re.findall(r"[a-zA-Z]|[^a-zA-Z]+", code_string)
    return "".join(result[:level])

Predicting Liver Hepatotoxicity Likelihood Score¶

A common problem with the administration of therapeutics is their toxicity. A drug that is effective in treating a given disease may need to be stopped prematurely if it is fatally damaging organs such as the liver or kidney. We have curated liver toxicity information from Liver Toxicity Knowledgebase which provides liver toxicity grade scores ranging from 0 to 8, with 8 being severe liver toxicity.

We first need to read in the label data and drop the compounds without a PubChem ID and without a label.

The accuracy here is poor. It could be that the modalities used to train Mol-JEPA are not as relevant for predicting cardiotoxicity. The layers used to train the Mol-JEPA model are mostly concentrated in molecular descriptors, with some of the training layers coming from foundation models that look at expression perturbation and cell painting (see Table 2 in the preprint). Future work will look at different modalities involved in creating the Mol-JEPA embeddings as well as adding additional layers to understand which modalities are most useful for each potential downstream task.
